# Product requirements v0.1

## Problem

Supply-chain, company, population, labor, household, fiscal, and financial impacts are usually analyzed separately. NEXUS WORLD links them into versioned world states and compares evidence-backed counterfactual policy branches.

## Primary user story

As a policy or supply-chain analyst, I can apply a Taiwan semiconductor production shock and compare no intervention, company subsidy, and unemployment-benefit/retraining/diversification policies across production, prices, jobs, disposable income, poverty, inequality, housing burden, and fiscal cost.

## Success criteria

- Every factual claim and model input has provenance or is labeled as an assumption.
- A seed and input version reproduce the same simulation state.
- Wage, tax, transfer, population, and household budget invariants reconcile.
- Parallel branches are isolated.
- External society APIs return aggregates, never synthetic person rows.
- The final scenario runs from a clean deployment and is explainable through evidence and traces.
