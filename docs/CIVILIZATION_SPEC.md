# Civilization specification v0.1

Korea is the detailed reference population with at most 20,000 households and approximately 55,000 synthetic persons. Taiwan, the United States, and Japan begin as weighted cohorts.

Official aggregate statistics are normalized, calibrated, and versioned. No real personal data is ingested. Public interfaces expose aggregates by approved dimensions and apply a small-cell suppression policy.

Citizen/household LLM behavior operates on at most 16 archetypes. A deterministic engine applies cached, bounded archetype policies to household state.
