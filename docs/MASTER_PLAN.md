# Master plan v0.1

NEXUS WORLD is a 56-day implementation of an evidence-grounded supply-chain digital twin coupled to a deterministic economic civilization simulation.

The reference scenario is a Taiwan semiconductor supply shock propagating through Korean production, employment, household income and consumption, followed by comparison of three policy branches.

## Delivery order

1. Foundation and deployment path
2. Provenance-aware real data and ontology
3. Retrieval and deterministic industrial simulation
4. Agent workflows and multimodal evidence
5. Synthetic population and household economy
6. Industry-to-society feedback
7. Reliability, security, evaluation, and operations

## Scope rules

- P0: evidence, ontology, deterministic simulation, Korean synthetic population, parallel worlds, evaluation, privacy.
- P1: multimodal RAG, multi-agent proposals, credit/housing, automation, observability, incident commander.
- P2: long-run demographics, detailed foreign populations, relocation, Monte Carlo uncertainty.
- Cut: real personal data, one LLM per citizen, unconstrained remediation, fine-tuning, EKS.
