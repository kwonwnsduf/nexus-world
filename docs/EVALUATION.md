# Evaluation baseline v0.1

Evaluation is split into text retrieval, GraphRAG, multimodal retrieval, agent/tool behavior, deterministic simulation, synthetic population, civilization policy, and security/incident cases.

Initial hard gates include repeatable seeded output, population conservation, wage/tax/transfer reconciliation, household budget validity, branch isolation, and zero row-level leakage from public APIs.

The simulator communicates scenarios and sensitivities; it does not claim precise prediction of future economic or market outcomes.
